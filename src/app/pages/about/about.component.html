Our background

Kolkata event planners with 10+ years of experience

Based in Ultadanga, Kolkata, Prime Event & Wedding is a trusted event management company specializing in weddings, engagements, corporate events, and private celebrations. For more than ten years, families and brands across West Bengal have relied on us to plan events that feel personal, polished, and stress-free.

Originality, customization, and attention to detail define our work. From destination weddings and birthday parties to corporate conferences and pre-wedding ceremonies, we handle planning, vendor coordination, and on-ground execution under one dedicated team.

Our client-first approach means transparent communication, realistic budgets, and reliable delivery — whether you need a single-function planner or full wedding management in Kolkata.
